# Phase 3 – Database Design

## Introduction

The database for the Car Wash Management System (CWMS) is designed using the relational database model and normalized to Third Normal Form (3NF). The design ensures data integrity, minimizes redundancy, and supports efficient storage and retrieval of information.

---

# Database Entities

The system consists of the following entities:

1. CUSTOMER
2. VEHICLE
3. EMPLOYEE
4. SERVICE
5. SERVICE_ORDER
6. ORDER_DETAIL
7. PAYMENT
8. PUBLIC_HOLIDAY
9. AUDIT_LOG

---

# Entity Descriptions

## CUSTOMER

Stores customer information.

**Attributes**

- customer_id (PK)
- first_name
- last_name
- phone
- email
- address

---

## VEHICLE

Stores customer vehicle information.

**Attributes**

- vehicle_id (PK)
- customer_id (FK)
- plate_number
- vehicle_type
- vehicle_model

---

## EMPLOYEE

Stores employee information.

**Attributes**

- employee_id (PK)
- first_name
- last_name
- position
- phone

---

## SERVICE

Stores all services offered by the car wash.

**Attributes**

- service_id (PK)
- service_name
- price

---

## SERVICE_ORDER

Stores customer service orders.

**Attributes**

- order_id (PK)
- customer_id (FK)
- vehicle_id (FK)
- employee_id (FK)
- order_date
- status

---

## ORDER_DETAIL

Stores the services included in each order.

**Attributes**

- detail_id (PK)
- order_id (FK)
- service_id (FK)
- quantity
- unit_price

---

## PAYMENT

Stores payment information.

**Attributes**

- payment_id (PK)
- order_id (FK)
- payment_date
- amount
- payment_method

---

## PUBLIC_HOLIDAY

Stores holidays used by business-rule triggers.

**Attributes**

- holiday_id (PK)
- holiday_name
- holiday_date

---

## AUDIT_LOG

Stores audit information generated by triggers.

**Attributes**

- audit_id (PK)
- table_name
- operation
- old_value
- new_value
- changed_by
- change_date

---

---

# Normalization

## First Normal Form (1NF)

- Atomic values only
- No repeating groups

## Second Normal Form (2NF)

- No partial dependency
- Every non-key attribute depends on the whole primary key

## Third Normal Form (3NF)

- No transitive dependency
- Every non-key attribute depends only on the primary key
