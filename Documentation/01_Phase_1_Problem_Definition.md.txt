# Phase 1 – Problem Definition

## Project Title

Car Wash Management System (CWMS)

## Introduction

The Car Wash Management System (CWMS) is designed to automate the daily operations of a car wash business. It replaces manual record-keeping with a centralized Oracle database that improves efficiency, accuracy, and accountability.

## Background

Many small and medium-sized car wash businesses still rely on paper records or spreadsheets. These methods often result in lost customer records, inaccurate billing, duplicate entries, and poor reporting. CWMS addresses these issues through a secure Oracle database system.

## Problem Statement

Manual management of customers, vehicles, services, and payments leads to inefficiencies, data inconsistency, and limited reporting capabilities. There is also no reliable audit trail for tracking database changes.

## General Objective

To develop an Oracle-based Car Wash Management System that automates business operations and improves data management.

## Specific Objectives

- Register customers and vehicles
- Manage available services
- Process service orders
- Record payments
- Generate reports
- Implement audit logging
- Enforce business rules using PL/SQL
