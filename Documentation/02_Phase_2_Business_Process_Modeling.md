# Phase 2 – Business Process Modeling

## System Actors

- Customer
- Receptionist
- Service Attendant
- Manager
- Database Administrator (DBA)

## Business Process

1. Customer arrives at the car wash.
2. Receptionist registers the customer and vehicle.
3. Receptionist creates a service order.
4. Service Attendant performs the selected services.
5. Order status is updated.
6. Payment is processed.
7. Audit logs are automatically recorded.
8. Manager views reports.

## In Scope

- Customer registration
- Vehicle registration
- Service management
- Order processing
- Payment processing
- Reporting
- Audit logging

## Out of Scope

- Online booking
- Inventory management
- Payroll
- Multi-branch synchronization
